# The MATE + TRIADA method (operational summary for StasisPath)

- **R1** preserve tension · **R2** invariance of rules (never move thresholds after the fact) · **R3** project the last three forced moves · **R4** critical squares (definition → unit → floor → impostor → claim) · **R5** mate ≠ theatre.
- **TRIADA, mandatory before reading or computing:** 1) INVENTORY: is it already in `es/10_fuentes/` or `es/30_respuestas/`? 2) MATHEMATICS: write the numerical bound, with numbers, in `es/20_cotas/` before reading anything. 3) MATE: what earlier reading or computation can be reused?
- **Reading rule:** from each paper, extract only the datum the bound needs (see the "extract" column in `es/10_fuentes/fuentes.md`). If the datum confirms the bound, the question moves to [M]/[V] and reading stops.
- **A leak means there is no mate:** every [M] lists explicitly its possible leaks and why they do not apply.
